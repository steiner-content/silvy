What advisors usually ask first.

If your question isn't here, bring it to the call.

How do you handle compliance?

Every workflow includes a compliance review step before anything publishes. We build your firm's compliance posture directly into the agent: disclaimers, prohibited language, citations, performance claim handling. Nothing ships without a human review against those rules.

For SEC-registered firms, we work with your CCO during Foundation to map the review chain. We are not a compliance vendor and don't replace your CCO.

Can the agents sound like our firm?

That's the whole point of Foundation. We spend 4–6 weeks profiling your voice through 25 video questions, client call transcripts, and existing content before any agent writes a word for you. That profile becomes the source of truth every agent references.

A senior editor reviews drafts before they go out. Month one is heavy on calibration. By month two, your editor approves output in minutes.

Why not just hire someone in-house?

You can. A senior marketer at an RIA runs $180–220K base plus benefits, so call it $250K+ loaded. They'll be capped by what one person can do in a day, take 3–6 months to recruit, and another 3–6 to ramp.

Silvy delivers the same capacity for ~$100K in year one. When the system is mature, hiring someone in-house to run it becomes a much easier conversation. We'll help you write the job description.

Why not just buy ChatGPT Enterprise and do this ourselves?

You can, and we'd rather you do that than hire the wrong agency. But raw access to a model isn't the same as a system. The lift is in the workflows: how the model is prompted, what it has access to, how outputs are reviewed, where compliance fits, what gets measured.

Silvy is the operating layer on top of those tools. If you want to take it in-house and cancel us, you can. We document everything.

Why is Maintain priced lower than Build?

Build is where the heavy lifting happens: designing the agents, writing the prompts, wiring the workflows, training them on your firm. By Maintain, that's done. Maintain is auditing, measuring, and rebuilding components when better tools ship.

If Maintain ever stops being worth $6K/mo, end it. The system is yours.

Do you do paid Google Ads, podcasts, video, or events?

Not in scope. We do SEO, content, email, LinkedIn organic and paid, funnel/CRO, and reporting. If your strategy needs Google Ads or video production, we'll point you to people who do that well and stay in our lane.
